# Kelvin catalogue research

Research snapshot: 14 November 2026. This file records competitor patterns used to structure Kelvin's website. Kelvin's own product information was approved by the project owner for the site; competitor material is not a substitute for Kelvin technical documents.

## Company and catalogue

- Kelvin was founded nine years ago and has an office in Dubai.
- Manufacturing is in Khalifa Economic Zones Abu Dhabi (KEZAD).
- The website presents 12 current products and 22 proposed products marked **Concept**. It does not take orders.
- The 12 current products appear in the home-page feature section. No bestseller ranking is claimed.

## Competitor patterns reviewed

| Source | Useful pattern | Kelvin direction |
|---|---|---|
| [Betco product taxonomy](https://www.betco.com/products/floor-care?pageNumber=1) | Floor care is divided by finish, sealer, stripper, maintenance, and surface type. | Organize Kelvin products by application and family. |
| [Betco floor-care selector](https://www.betco.com/solutions/floor-care) | Product selection changes by floor substrate and maintenance stage. | Help visitors browse by task. |
| [Betco public product list](https://www.betco.com/productlist.html) | Laundry and warewashing have distinct product families. | Keep these families easy to find. |
| [Intercare specialty chemicals](https://www.intercare-ltd.com/specialty-chemicals) | Regional manufacturing sites explain facilities and quality processes. | Describe Kelvin's own operation with Kelvin-supplied evidence. |
| [Ecolab About](https://www.ecolab.com/en-us/about) | Connects products to customer settings and support. | Explain the range through the buyer's application in Kelvin's own language. |

The competitor examples supplied during research remain reference material. Kelvin's product names, codes, artwork, and descriptions are maintained in [src/catalogue.json](src/catalogue.json) and `public/products/`. Product directions, hazard information, and specifications should be checked against the matching Kelvin technical documents.
