Research snapshot: 14 November 2026. This file guides an internal website concept. It does **not** establish Kelvin's actual formulations, product approvals, packaging sizes, or sales rankings.

- Kelvin was founded nine years ago.
- Its office is in Dubai.
- Its manufacturing site is in Khalifa Economic Zones Abu Dhabi (KEZAD).

These statements came from the project owner in this chat. Confirm the legal entity, facility address, and wording before public launch.

## Catalogue and feature decisions

- Build 32 **concept entries**: 14 floor-care, 7 laundry, 5 warewashing, and 6 surface/restroom concepts.
- Each card shows a transparent AI-generated Kelvin concept packshot directly on the site background, an application category, and a short non-technical description.
- No dilution ratio, ingredient, hazard, efficacy, certification, food-contact, or disinfectant registration claim should be invented.
- No verifiable Betco or Kelvin bestseller ranking was found. The homepage's featured products are **editorial concept picks** spanning floor cleaning, floor finish, and laundry. They must not be labeled “best-selling.”
- Product names, exact packaging, label copy, specifications, SDS/TDS, and usage directions require Kelvin's approval before launch.
