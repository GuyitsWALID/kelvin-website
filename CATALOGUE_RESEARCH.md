# Kelvin catalogue research

Research snapshot: 19 November 2026. This file records competitor patterns used to structure Kelvin's website. Kelvin's original product information was approved by the project owner for the site; competitor material is not a substitute for Kelvin technical documents.

## Company and catalogue

- Kelvin was founded nine years ago and has an office in Dubai.
- Manufacturing is in Khalifa Economic Zones Abu Dhabi (KEZAD).
- The website presents 12 current products and 36 additional catalogue products. It does not take orders.
- The 12 current products appear in the home-page feature section. No bestseller ranking is claimed.

## November expansion

The 14 new entries are `KCC-601`–`KCC-604`, `KFP-701`–`KFP-704`, `KLS-801`–`KLS-802`, and `KWT-901`–`KWT-904`. They fill chemical-category gaps in the original 34-product catalogue. Competitor SKU names, logos, formulations, pack sizes, dilution rates, certifications, and performance claims were not copied. Equipment, monitoring software, consulting, and services shown in the reference navigation were not represented as Kelvin products. The project owner approved the new entries and packshots for the website. Formulations, safety data, application suitability, and local registration where required must be checked against Kelvin's product-specific technical documents before use.

The competitor examples supplied during research remain reference material. Kelvin's product names, codes, artwork, and descriptions are maintained in [src/catalogue.json](src/catalogue.json) and `public/products/`. Product directions, hazard information, and specifications should be checked against the matching Kelvin technical documents.
